The Long Swim

Choose a sawfish, hammerhead shark or sea turtle and swim home through the fishing grounds: dodge baited hooks, drifting ghost nets and plastic bags, keep your belly full, and reach the lagoon where your family is waiting.

  • 3 playable endangered animals, each with a real life challenge
  • 4 difficulties, from Sheltered Bay to the Extreme Gauntlet
  • Real Did you know? facts about bycatch and ocean plastic
  • Works on desktop, tablet and phone, with a touch joystick
