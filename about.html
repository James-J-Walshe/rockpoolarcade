The person behind the rockpool

Meet Mia

Grade 6 student. Queenslander. Frequent worrier about oceans, occasional befriender of koalas.

Her story

Mia is a grade 6 student in Queensland, Australia, and she's worried about the environment — properly worried, the kind of worried that turns into doing something about it.

It started with a school research project about four sea animals that look nothing alike but are all disappearing for the same reasons: a sawfish, a hawksbill turtle, a hammerhead shark, and a tiny porpoise called the vaquita. The more she learned, the more she wanted other kids to know about it too — but nobody remembers a fact sheet. Everybody remembers a game they almost won.

So Rockpool Arcade was born: little games about big oceans, built from her research. The dangers in the games are real. So is the good news at the end — because protecting these animals actually works, wherever people choose to do it.

When she's not worrying about oceans, Mia can be found in eucalyptus territory. Which brings us to…

🐨

The koala department

Mia's favourite animal isn't in the sea at all — it's the koala. Her favourite place to visit is the Daisy Hill Koala Centre, set in beautiful bushland in Daisy Hill Conservation Park near Brisbane.

It's free, it's open 10am–4pm every day, and it's full of interactive displays about how to keep koalas safe — plus resident koalas to meet. A very big shout-out from Mia: if you're ever nearby, go. 💚
